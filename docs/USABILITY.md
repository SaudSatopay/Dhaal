# Usability protocol — Hindi/Hinglish participants (PREPARED, NOT YET RUN)

**Status: no sessions have been conducted. Nothing below reports results —
running this protocol is pending work.** No participants, quotes, success
rates, or prevented losses exist and none may be claimed until sessions
actually happen. The /learn practice flow uses clearly fictional scenarios,
collects no credentials or payments, and its tallies stay on-device only.

## Purpose

Measure whether a Hindi/Hinglish-speaking person facing a Dhaal warning can:
1. name the ACTION the sender wants (send money / share a code / open a link
   / approve a request),
2. state the MONEY DIRECTION (out of my account / to me / can't tell),
3. recognize UNCERTAINTY (the app is asking me something, not clearing me),
4. choose a sensible NEXT STEP (refuse + verify independently / ask trusted
   person / 1930 / proceed carefully).

## Participants & consent

- 5–8 adults comfortable in Hindi or Hinglish; include at least 2 aged 45+.
- Verbal consent script (Hindi), stating: fictional scenarios only; no real
  money, credentials or accounts; participation is voluntary and stoppable at
  any time; only anonymous aggregates recorded (no names, numbers, or
  recordings unless separately and explicitly agreed).
- Data collected per task: completion (Y/N), the four comprehension answers
  (correct/incorrect/unsure), time-to-first-correct-explanation (stopwatch,
  from warning shown to participant correctly stating the requested action),
  points of confusion (facilitator notes, no verbatim PII).

## Tasks (fixed order, ~15 min)

1. **Paste task:** hand a printed fictional KYC scam SMS; participant pastes
   it in /check and reads the result. Ask questions 1–4.
2. **X-Ray task:** ask them to tap one highlight and explain in their own
   words why that phrase matters.
3. **QR task:** fictional "refund" pay-QR (demo/qr_collect_15000.png) with
   the receive expectation. Ask money direction before AND after the verdict.
4. **Uncertainty task:** paste a bare phone number; observe whether they
   understand a question came back, and answer it via the chips.
5. **Practice flow:** one /learn round; note whether feedback reads as
   neutral (never scolding) to the participant.

## Measures & reporting

- Aggregate only: % correct per question per task, median time-to-understand,
  count of confusion points by screen. No individual rows leave the room.
- Report failures verbatim as design findings (e.g. "3/6 read the score as a
  probability") — the point is to find them.
- A practice score is NOT a safety score and is never presented as one.
